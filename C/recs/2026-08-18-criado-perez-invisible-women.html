Invisible Women

Caroline Criado Perez · 2019 · “Exposing Data Bias in a World Designed for Men”

Crash-test dummies built to male bodies. Drug trials that skip female subjects. Phones too big for the average woman's hand, and cities snow-ploughed in an order that assumes a male commute. This book collects the receipts — hundreds of studies — for something your feminism wall keeps saying in theory: the default human is male, and the data proves it.

The honest fine print

It will make you angry, by design

Honesty guard

Three warnings. It is statistics-dense — story after story after number; brilliant in chapters, relentless in one sitting. It was published in 2019, so a handful of examples have since improved (partly because of this book). And its lens is resolutely binary — men's data vs women's data — which some of your B3 authors would themselves push back on. None of that dents the core argument; it tells you how to read it.
